Inventory movement screen for the running turn: from **TURNO → INVENTARIO** the operator sees, product by product, what the turn received, what came in, what was sold, what was given as courtesy and what is left.

**Changes for this version**

- **New INVENTARIO screen**: a read-only table of the active turn with *Artículo*, *Recibido*, *Entrada*, *Total Ingresado*, *Ventas*, *Cortesías* and *Existencias actuales*. It is opened from the turn summary and *VOLVER* returns there; the clock and date update like in the other screens.
- **What each column means**: *Recibido* is the stock at the start of the turn (the same "recibido" the printed report already shows), *Entrada* is the manual increases of the turn (restock and product creation), *Total Ingresado* is both added together, *Ventas* and *Cortesías* split the sold units by whether they had a price, and *Existencias actuales* is the live stock read when the screen opens.
- **What is not a column**: sale refunds, consumptions and deletions do not have a column of their own, so the current stock is the real stock after all of them and the row does not have to add up exactly. Column order and visibility customization is left for a later version.
- **Deleted and renamed products**: a product deleted during the turn stays in the table with current stock 0, and a renamed product is shown under its current name. A turn written before inventory traceability existed rebuilds *Recibido* from the tracked movements instead of showing a zero.

**Data**: no file-format change — `data/turn` and `history/` stay at `"version": 4` and `data/inventory` at `"version": 3`. The screen only reads the opening/closing snapshots, the manual movements and the sale activities already stored; no quantity is rewritten.

**Verified with** 654 tests.

**Requirements and how to run**

- **Java 25**
- Only this `.jar` is needed: it is a self-contained build, every image, message bundle and factory template ships inside it
- `java -jar Motel_management-0.1.6.3.jar`. The working directories (`data/`, `backup/`, `history/`, `receiptPrints/`, `logs/`) are created next to the jar on first run
- **Updating an existing installation**: copy its `data/` directory next to this jar as it is. There is no conversion step
