Inventory traceability: each product has a minimum, and the turn records its stock movements and the opening and closing snapshots they produce.

**Changes for this version**

- **Inventory screen**: each product has a *MÍNIMO* — the quantity at or below which it is reported as low. `0` (the default) means only an empty product is reported.
- **Movements**: an inventory increase (*AUMENTO DE INVENTARIO*) or a consumption (*CONSUMO DE INVENTARIO*) is recorded with the concept typed by the user; an increase also takes the total cost. Sales, courtesy sales and refunds stay in the turn activity, and the reports read them from there.
- **Turn**: `data/turn` keeps the movements and an opening and a closing stock snapshot; each archive in `history/` keeps the same information.
- **Reports**: the turn summary and detail — including the built-in layout — print the inventory section, with Surtido, Vendido, Consumido and Devuelto totals and `[BAJO]` / `[AGOTADO]` marks.
- **Selling**: the sale screen shows the stock of each product and warns when it is low or out, without blocking the sale.

**Data**: additive and backward-compatible. `data/inventory` goes to `version: 3` with an optional `minimum`; `data/turn` and `history/` go to `version: 4` with `inventoryMovements`, `openingInventory` and `closingInventory`. An older data directory loads as it is: no quantity is migrated or rewritten, an absent minimum reads as `0` and absent lists as empty. Existing negative stock is preserved and reported as out until the first real count is recorded as a movement.

**Verified with** 628 tests; the jar builds and starts.

**Requirements and how to run**

- **Java 25**
- The build is self-contained: every image, message bundle and factory template ships inside the jar
- The `.jar` of this family is published on [v0.1.6.1](https://github.com/TheAbsdag/Motel_management/releases/tag/v0.1.6.1); this exact version can be rebuilt from its tag with `.\mvnw.cmd clean package`. Run it with `java -jar Motel_management-0.1.6.0.jar`; the working directories (`data/`, `backup/`, `history/`, `receiptPrints/`, `logs/`) are created next to the jar on first run
- **Updating an existing installation**: copy its `data/` directory next to this jar as it is. There is no conversion step
